Git Pulse watches the repositories you work in and puts what matters in the top bar: the current branch, whether CI is green and who is waiting on your review.

## What it does

- Supports GitHub, GitLab and Forgejo
- Notifications for failed pipelines and new review requests
- Click a status to open the run in your browser
