Semantic Search adds a meaning-aware index to the launcher. Type "receipt from the Lisbon trip" and it finds the PDF, the photo and the email thread, even when none of them use those words.

## What it does

- Builds embeddings in the background while you are idle, on battery only when plugged in
- Reads text inside images and scanned PDFs
- Keeps the whole index encrypted in your home directory

## Permissions

| Scope | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Files (read) | Indexes the folders you choose |
| Local Models | Generates embeddings on device |
