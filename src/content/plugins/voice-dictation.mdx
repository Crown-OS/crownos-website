Hold the dictation key and speak. Voice Dictation streams audio to a local Whisper model and types the result into whatever field has focus, with punctuation and casing handled for you.

## What it does

- Works in every app through the input method framework
- Ninety-nine languages, switched automatically
- Custom vocabulary for names and jargon

## Permissions

| Scope | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Microphone | Only while the key is held |
| Input method | Types the transcription |
