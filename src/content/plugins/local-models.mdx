Local Models pulls, quantises and serves open-weight models through a single system daemon. Crown Agent and any plugin that asks for a model get it over a local socket, so prompts and files never leave the machine.

## What it does

- Detects your GPU and picks the right backend: CUDA, ROCm or Vulkan
- Downloads models on demand and shares one copy between apps
- Unloads idle models after a timeout to free VRAM
- Exposes an OpenAI-compatible endpoint on `localhost` for your own tools

## Permissions

| Scope | Why |
| --- | --- |
| GPU | Runs inference on your graphics card |
| Network (opt-in) | Downloads model weights |
