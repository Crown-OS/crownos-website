Start a session from the panel and CrownOS holds everything else back: notification sync pauses, chosen apps are hidden from the launcher and your phone switches to do not disturb through Crown Link.

## What it does

- Pomodoro or free-form sessions with a ring in the top bar
- Per-session allow lists for people and apps
- A weekly summary of focused hours
