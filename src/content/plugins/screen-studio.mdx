Screen Studio captures through PipeWire and encodes on the GPU, so recording a 4K display costs almost nothing.

## What it does

- AV1, HEVC or H.264 via VA-API and NVENC
- Smooth zoom that follows clicks and typing
- Exports straight to a share link with Quick File Sharing
