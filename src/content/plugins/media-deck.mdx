Media Deck gathers every MPRIS player and anything playing on a linked phone into one panel. Skip, scrub and move audio between speakers without hunting for the right window.

## What it does

- Synced lyrics where the source provides them
- Output switching across PipeWire sinks and Bluetooth devices
- Keyboard media keys routed to the last active player
