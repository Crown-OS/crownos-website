---
title: Introduction
description: Crownpaper is the GPU wallpaper daemon for CrownOS and other Wayland desktops.
---

Crownpaper draws the desktop background on every output. It shows still images and video, and it
moves between wallpapers with a cross-blur transition rendered on the GPU. When the screen is at rest
the renderer is released, so the daemon costs almost nothing while you work.

## What it supports

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Still wallpapers" icon="image">
    JPEG, PNG, WebP and BMP, decoded once and cached for instant changes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Video wallpapers" icon="zap">
    VA-API hardware decoding in a separate `crownpaper-video` process.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GPU transitions" icon="sparkles">
    A cross-blur between wallpapers, rendered with wgpu on Vulkan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Typed IPC" icon="code">
    One protocol definition generates the daemon handler and every client.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it stays light

- The GPU renderer is released shortly after a transition ends.
- FFmpeg is loaded only while a video wallpaper plays, inside its own process.
- Wallpapers you are about to show can be preloaded so `set` never waits on a decoder.

<Tip>
  Crownpaper runs on any compositor with `wlr-layer-shell`, `wp_viewporter` and `linux-dmabuf`,
  such as Hyprland, Sway and Niri — not only on CrownOS.
</Tip>
