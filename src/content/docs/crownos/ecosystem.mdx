title: Ecosystem

---

Crown Link is the encrypted peer-to-peer protocol that pairs CrownOS with your phone, tablet and
remote machines. Clipboard, notifications, files, calls and running agents move between them
without a cloud in the middle.

## Pair a device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the companion app">
    Install CrownConnect on your Android phone or tablet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Crown Link on the desktop">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Crown Link**. Nearby devices that run the companion app are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the pairing">
    Select the device and accept the request on both screens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Pairing is end-to-end encrypted. Traffic between paired devices never passes through a CrownOS
  server.
</Note>

## Choose what to share

Every feature can be turned on or off per device in **Settings → Crown Link**. A device you
unpair loses access to all of them immediately.
